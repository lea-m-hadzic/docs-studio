# Attribution

`docs-studio` is licensed under **Apache-2.0**. The styling it generates derives from
two other projects, and that derivation comes with conditions — which matter to you,
because publishing a generated site redistributes the derived material.

The short version: **it is handled, and you do not have to do anything.**

## What derives from what

| Source | License | What was derived |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [sisl/ARIS.jl](https://github.com/sisl/ARIS.jl) | MIT, © 2025 hdelecki and contributors | the stylesheet structure, the `indigo-lavender` palette, parts of the checker and the Pages workflow |
| [sisl/wm-physics-eval](https://github.com/sisl/wm-physics-eval) | Apache-2.0 | the stylesheet structure, the `ocean-violet` palette, the Pages workflow |

Both licenses permit redistribution outright. Neither needs anyone's further
permission. What they require is attribution, and a statement of what changed.

## How your site satisfies them

The generated `docs/stylesheets/extra.css` opens with an attribution header carrying:

- ARIS.jl's MIT copyright notice and permission notice, in full, as MIT requires;
- the Apache-2.0 notice, warranty disclaimer and a link to the license text;
- a statement of the changes made, as Apache-2.0 section 4(b) requires;
- a link to `docs-studio`'s complete `NOTICE`.

It is in the stylesheet rather than beside it on purpose: the CSS is the derived
artifact, so a notice inside it cannot be separated from the thing it describes. It
survives the build and is served with your site.

!!! info "`check` watches it for you"
    Remove the header and `docs-studio check` reports `attribution-removed` as a
    warning. The stylesheet is also [managed](ownership.md), so an edit to it shows up
    as a conflict before an update can quietly restore or lose anything.

## What you might add

Nothing is required. If you want the notices easier to find than inside a stylesheet,
put a `NOTICE` at your repository root:

```text
<your project>

Portions of the documentation styling are generated by docs-studio
(https://github.com/lea-m-hadzic/docs-studio, Apache-2.0), and derive from the
documentation sites of sisl/ARIS.jl (MIT) and sisl/wm-physics-eval (Apache-2.0).
The full notices are in docs/stylesheets/extra.css.
```

`check` suggests this once, as a note, and stops once any of `NOTICE`,
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` or `ATTRIBUTION` exists.

## Your own content

Your pages, your artwork and `docs/stylesheets/custom.css` are yours, under whatever
license you choose. Nothing in `docs-studio`'s license reaches them — it is a
generator, and Apache-2.0 does not propagate to the content you write. Only the
generated styling carries the obligations above.

## Icons and fonts

None are redistributed. `simple-*`, `lucide/*` and `material/*` shortcodes resolve to
icons shipped with Zensical's theme at build time, and generated sites set
`font = false` and use system fonts — which is also why they make no third-party
request.
