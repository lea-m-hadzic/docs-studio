**A route planner for the city's protected bike lanes.**

Greenline plans a route that stays on protected lanes wherever one exists, and tells you
plainly where it could not. Every other planner we tried would happily route us down an
arterial road with a painted stripe and call it a bike route; this one treats that as a
gap and says so.

The lane data is maintained by volunteers. It is a map of what is actually there,
including the bollard that has been missing since February.

Maintained by Greenline Volunteers. *This is an example site generated by
`docs-studio`; the project is fictional.*

[Installation](install.md){ .md-button }

```bash
greenline route "Harbour Market" "Western Library"
```

```text
Harbour Market → Western Library
  4.1 km, 17 min, 3.4 km protected (83%)

  1. Quay Path, 1.2 km                       protected
  2. Mill Street bike lane, 0.9 km           protected
  3. Fifth Avenue, 0.7 km                    UNPROTECTED — painted lane only
  4. Western Greenway, 1.3 km                protected

  1 unprotected segment. `greenline route --avoid-unprotected` finds a way around.
```

!!! info "What counts as protected"
    A lane is protected when it is physically separated from motor traffic — a kerb, a
    planter, a row of bollards, or its own path. Paint is not separation. The full
    rule, and the handful of edge cases the volunteers argued about, is on
    [Adding a lane](adding-a-lane.md#what-counts-as-protected).

## What it does

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Routes that admit their gaps**

    ---

    A route is reported segment by segment, with every unprotected stretch named. You
    can also ask for a protected-only route and be told when there isn't one.
    See [Planning a route](usage.md).

-   **A map that gets corrected**

    ---

    A blocked lane is reported in one command and shows up in everyone's routing after
    the next data refresh. Blockages expire, so nothing silently stays broken forever.
    See [Reporting a blockage](blockages.md).

</div>

## Scope

!!! warning "One city, and no live traffic"
    The lane data covers this city only. Greenline does not know about traffic,
    weather, roadworks it has not been told about, or anything on the other side of
    the river that is not in the volunteer map.

It is also not a navigation app. There is no turn-by-turn voice, no GPS tracking, and
nothing that runs on a phone while you ride — by design, because none of the volunteers
wanted to maintain that.
